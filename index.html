🖥️ All Eaglercraft Clients
Modern Client
The all-rounder. Packed with HUD overlays, Toggle Sprint, FPS boosts and a sleek custom UI. Best for players who want a polished, feature-rich experience.
Tuff Client
A solid, stable client focused on performance and reliability. Great for players who want a clean, lag-free experience without unnecessary extras.
EB Client
Combat-focused client built for PvP. Includes hitbox enhancements, reach indicators and fast-paced combat tweaks to give you an edge in fights.
Resent Client
Known for its clean design and powerful features. Resent offers advanced PvP tools, smooth animations and a competitive HUD ideal for ranked play.
Death Client
The most feature-packed client. Includes advanced modules like KillAura, AutoFish, Velocity, Fly and more — for players who want full control.
Aero Client
Lightweight and ultra-fast. Aero is optimised for maximum FPS and minimal input lag — perfect for competitive players who prioritise raw performance.

🆕 Game Drop June 16, 2026
Chaos Cubed — The Sulfur Cube Update

The Minecraft "Chaos Cubed" update releases on June 16, 2026, introducing the brand-new Sulfur Cube mob alongside underground and rare surface Sulfur Caves. Sulfur Cubes can absorb different blocks to take on their physics — bouncing, sliding, or becoming heavy — and can even be transported in buckets.

🟡 Sulfur Cube Mobs

  • Passive mobs that spawn naturally inside Sulfur Caves
  • Feeding them a block changes their behaviour and physical properties — wool makes them float, netherite makes them sink
  • Can be sheared, leashed, and fed slime balls to grow larger
  • Pick them up and carry them in buckets just like axolotls
  • Take increased knockback when hit with enchanted weapons

🌋 Sulfur Caves & Springs

  • A brand-new biome spanning deep underground and occasionally breaking through to the surface
  • Features erupting geysers — potent sulfur reacting violently with magma blocks
  • Noxious gas effects, Cave Spider spawns, and new block sets including Cinnabar and Sulfur blocks
  • Geysers properly emit vibrations, triggering sculk sensors in the area

🔧 Pre-Release 4 Tweaks

  • Sulfur Cubes now take increased knockback from enchanted weapons
  • Geysers correctly emit sculk sensor vibrations
  • Polished mob collision logic and refined block textures across both Java and Bedrock

The Chaos Cubed update is shaping up to be one of the most unique drops in Minecraft history. Whether you're exploring deep caves or taming your own physics-defying cube companion, there's something new for everyone.
